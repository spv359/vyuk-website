# Website enquiry retention

The published Privacy Policy states this company policy. The current website stores enquiries in `discovery_requests`; it does not implement a scheduled deletion job. Retention therefore requires a manual review and deletion process until automated cleanup is separately implemented.

The team responsible for hello@vyukai.in should review routine enquiries before their 12-month deadline, handle access/correction/deletion requests, and keep only records that have a documented client or legal retention reason. Do not assume that changing the Privacy Policy deletes database records.
